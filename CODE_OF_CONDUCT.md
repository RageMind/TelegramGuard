# Code of Conduct

TelegramGuard follows a simple standard: collaborate professionally, discuss technical decisions on their merits, and do not publish another person's private infrastructure or credentials.

Harassment, doxxing, credential disclosure, deliberate sabotage, and publication of exploit details against live systems are not acceptable.

Security reports belong in the private reporting path described in SECURITY.md.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that creates a security or privacy risk.
